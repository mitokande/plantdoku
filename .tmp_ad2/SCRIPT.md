## Voiceover

Quick brain teaser: where can this last plant go? Plantdoku gives you three rules. Exactly one plant in every row and column. Exactly one in every colored patch. And plants can’t touch, even diagonally. Mark impossible cells with X’s. Spot the forced move. Made a mistake? Undo it or take a hint. Easy starts at six by six. Hard goes nine by nine. Can your brain solve today’s board?
