# Gameplay beat sheet

1. 0–4s — Nearly solved real-style board; highlight last empty cell. Brain-teaser hook.
2. 4–9s — Board row and column sweep; place one plant.
3. 9–13s — Colored patch outline; show one-per-color rule.
4. 13–17s — Illegal diagonal placement turns red and shakes.
5. 17–22s — Rapid X-marking, undo, and hint pulse on the board.
6. 22–26s — Difficulty jumps 6×6 → 8×8 → 9×9 while board stays central.
7. 26–30s — Final plant lands, progress completes, win burst, logo CTA.
